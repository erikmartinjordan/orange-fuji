01 · Features

Capture anything, annotate fast.

Region, window, or fullscreen — then mark it up and share.

Annotation

Arrows, boxes, circles, text, freehand, highlights, and blur. Clean marks, no clutter.

Region capture

Click and drag any area. Precision guides and zoomed loupe included.

Window capture

Capture any open window with one click, or scroll-enabled full-page shots.

Screen recording

Record with system audio on Windows. Trim, loop, and export to MP4 or GIF.

Copy & share

Auto-copies to clipboard. Paste anywhere. Drag to save. Share in seconds.

Global shortcuts

Cmd+Shift+S to capture. Switch modes, save, or copy — all from the keyboard.
